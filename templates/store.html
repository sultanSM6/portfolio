What's Included

Product Catalogue

A clean, filterable product grid with photos, names, descriptions, and prices. Customers browse your full range beautifully — on any device.

WhatsApp Order Button

Every product card has a "Order via WhatsApp" button that pre-fills the product name in the message. No payment gateway needed — simple and free.

Promotions & Offers Banner

A bold promotional banner for sales, seasonal offers, or new arrivals — visible at the top of the page so no customer misses your best deals.

QR Code for In-Store

A printable QR code that links to your online catalogue — place it on your counter, packaging, or window so customers can browse anytime.

Store Location & Hours

Google Maps embed with your exact location, opening hours per day, and holiday notices — so customers always know when and where to find you.

Customer Reviews

A social proof section with your best Google reviews — ratings, names, and comments that convince new customers to choose your store over competitors.

Instagram Product Feed

Link your Instagram feed directly to the website so your latest product photos stay fresh and encourage customers to follow and share.

Fully Responsive

Fast and beautiful on every device. Most customers discover local shops on their phones — your site needs to work perfectly on mobile.

Local SEO

Schema markup for local businesses, product keywords, and Google Maps optimisation to help people find your store in local search results.
