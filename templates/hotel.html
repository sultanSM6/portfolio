What's Included

Room Showcase

Beautiful room cards with photos, descriptions, bed types, capacity, amenities list, and a price-per-night display. Guests browse and decide before they even call.

Booking Request Form

Check-in / check-out date picker, room type selector, number of guests, and special requests — all sent directly to your email or WhatsApp instantly.

Amenities Section

Icon-based amenities grid — pool, spa, gym, restaurant, free WiFi, parking, airport transfer — presented visually so guests see the full value at a glance.

Full Photo Gallery

High-impact image gallery of rooms, common areas, views, and dining. Lightbox zoom. Lazy-loaded for fast performance even on mobile connections.

Location & Attractions

Google Maps embed plus a curated list of nearby attractions — beaches, museums, restaurants, transport links — to help guests plan their stay.

Guest Reviews

Showcase your best reviews from Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or Google directly on your site — star ratings, guest names, and dates included.

WhatsApp Booking

A floating WhatsApp button with a pre-filled "I'd like to book a room" message — the fastest way for guests to confirm availability with you directly.

Travel SEO
